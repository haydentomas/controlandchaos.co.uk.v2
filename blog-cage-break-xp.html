Cage Break Arena: Double XP Weekend Event

Join us this Friday through Sunday for high-stakes prisoner rescue battles with double XP and bonus Gold Coin rewards.

High-Stakes Prison Infiltration & Defense

The sirens are blaring across the lower levels of Control & Chaos! This Friday through Sunday, the Cage Break Combat Arena is awarding 2x XP and +500 Gold Coins for all jailbreaks, lock-picking hacks, and guard defenses.

Tournament Schedule & Rewards

  • Friday 6:00 PM SLT: Qualifying hacker trials & cage lock tests.
  • Saturday 1:00 PM SLT: The Championship Knockout Bracket.
  • Sunday 3:00 PM SLT: Faction War Free-For-All.

Every hour, the top scoring dominant and escapee will activate a 30-minute sim-wide XP boost for all players on the region. Bring your best team and gear up at the armory!
