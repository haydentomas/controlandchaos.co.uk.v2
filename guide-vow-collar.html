2. Quick Start & Locking Protocols

Step 1: Wear the Collar & Accept Permissions

Right-click > Add the collar. When prompted to accept Experience permissions for 'Control & Chaos', click Yes.

Step 2: Lock Ceremony & Passkey Setup

Touch the collar or type /22 menu. Select Lock Collar. The wearer or Mistress sets a private 4-digit passkey.

Step 3: Direct Debit & Air Tax Activation

In the FinDom > Air Tax menu, the submissive grants debit permission. The Mistress can configure a daily or per-minute tax.
