2. Custom Domain Setup

Step 1: Enter the domain you own above and click "Save Profile". Saving your profile does not automatically register the domain with Netlify.
Step 2: Contact Control & Chaos with your domain and Second Life username. We must add your domain as an alias on our Netlify site before it can work. For a root domain, tell us whether you also want www connected. Wait for our confirmation and DNS instructions.
Step 3: After confirmation, open your domain provider's "DNS Settings" or "DNS Management". For a root domain such as yourbrand.com, use the records below. If C&C support supplies different values from Netlify's verification screen, use those instead.
TYPENAME / HOSTTARGET / VALUETTL
A@75.2.60.5 Auto / 1 hr
CNAMEwwwheartfelt-centaur-ab06af.netlify.app Auto / 1 hr

Subdomain only: For vip.yourbrand.com, use one CNAME with host vip and target heartfelt-centaur-ab06af.netlify.app. You do not need to change the root domain's records. Ask C&C to register that exact subdomain.

Important: Do not use a normal CNAME at @ or point the root domain at controlandchaos.co.uk. Replace conflicting root A/AAAA/CNAME records only as instructed; keep unrelated email MX and TXT records. Providers supporting ALIAS, ANAME, or flattened CNAME may instead use apex-loadbalancer.netlify.com for the root, when confirmed by C&C support.

📖 Click-by-Click Guides for Popular Registrars:
🟡 GoDaddy Step-by-Step
1. Go to My Products → Click DNS next to your domain.
2. Click Add New Record.
3. Add or update an A record: Name @, Value 75.2.60.5, TTL 1 Hour.
4. Add or update a CNAME: Name www, Value heartfelt-centaur-ab06af.netlify.app.
5. Click Save. For a subdomain-only setup, add just the CNAME using your subdomain label instead of www.
🟠 Namecheap Step-by-Step
1. Go to Domain List → Click Manage next to your domain.
2. Click the Advanced DNS tab.
3. Add or update an A Record: Host @, Value 75.2.60.5, TTL Automatic.
4. Add or update a CNAME Record: Host www, Target heartfelt-centaur-ab06af.netlify.app.
5. Click the green checkmark to save each record. For a subdomain-only setup, add just the CNAME with that subdomain label.
🔵 Cloudflare Step-by-Step
1. Select your domain → Click DNS → Records.
2. Click Add record.
3. Add an A record: Name @, IPv4 address 75.2.60.5.
4. Add a CNAME: Name www, Target heartfelt-centaur-ab06af.netlify.app.
5. Keep both records DNS Only (Grey Cloud) during Netlify verification and HTTPS setup, then save. For a subdomain-only setup, add just the CNAME with that subdomain label.
⏳ Step 4: Allow several hours for DNS propagation; it can take up to 24 hours. C&C will confirm Netlify DNS verification and HTTPS certificate activation. Until then, "Pending DNS verification" or a certificate warning can appear. Your custom domain is ready only when those checks are complete; your C&C profile remains available throughout.
