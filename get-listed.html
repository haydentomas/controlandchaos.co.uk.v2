Frequently Asked Questions

Common Creator Inquiries

Do you take any percentage or commission from my client tributes?

Never. You keep 100% of every tribute, session fee, and tip you earn. We only charge a flat monthly listing fee to maintain the high-traffic ecosystem and web infrastructure.

How do I update my rates, photos, or hard limits?

Upon activation, you receive a private Studio Editor link. You can update your bio, rates, lookbook galleries, Lovense badges, and availability status 24/7 without needing staff assistance.

How does the Custom Vanity Domain work for VIP listings?

With the VIP Featured tier, you can point your own domain (e.g. mistressalexis.com) to our high-speed edge network. Your site gets automatic SSL and will load without the Control & Chaos platform header for a 100% private branded look.

What happens when my 30-day listing expires?

You can pay the in-world Kiosk anytime to add 30 days. If a subscription lapses, your profile is simply set to private until renewed; your rates and lookbook data are never lost.
