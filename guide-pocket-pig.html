1. Overview & Concept

The [C&C] PocketPig is an interactive gamified companion HUD and avatar accessory designed specifically for Financial Submissives (FinSubs) and Dominants. It tracks 'wallet hunger', forces auditory squealing when tributes are neglected, and broadcasts live coin tributes sim-wide.

Key Features:
  • Hunger & Drain Meter: Every minute without feeding Mistress increases hunger percentage. At 100%, the pig squeals and locks into crawl posture.
  • Tip-to-Feed Integration: Direct integration with Mistress's Tribute Jar or direct L$ payment instantly resets hunger.
  • Snout & Trotters Auto-Rezzing: Optional Bento props auto-attach via Experience permissions.
  • Vow Collar Link: Seamlessly syncs with the Vow Collar for compounded XP bonuses.

2. Quick Start & Pairing Mistress

Step 1: Wear the PocketPig HUD Add the HUD to any screen attachment point (Bottom Right recommended). Accept the Experience request for auto-animations.

Step 2: Assign Owner / Mistress Click the HUD > Pair Mistress > Click nearby Dominant avatar or enter their SL username. They receive an instant authorization request.

Step 3: Set Feed Interval & Squeal Volume Mistress configures how frequently the submissive must tribute (e.g. L$100 every 15 minutes) and sets sound volume.

3. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does PocketPig award Control & Chaos XP?
Yes! Every feed session awards +10 XP per L$100 to the submissive and +15 XP to the receiving Dominant on the sim leaderboard.

Q: Can it be worn without a Mistress?
Yes, in 'Self-Discipline' mode where hunger feeds into the public Sim Tribute Pool at Control & Chaos. Go get it!
